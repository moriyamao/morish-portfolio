# CATALYST original artwork

Created for this project with the built-in image generation tool. These are
AI-generated raster assets, not downloaded third-party stock. Original outputs
are preserved in the user's generated_images folder. PNG alpha is preserved
for the hand and flask. No third-party attribution is required by an asset pack.

## Prompt specifications

- `lab-background.png`: Production 3:2 first-person pixel chemistry laboratory;
  navy/slate walls, amber pendant, teal accents, wooden counter, moonlit window,
  reagent shelves and old analyzer; center counter empty for an animated flask;
  crisp pixel clusters; no hands, central vessel, text, UI or watermark.
- `gloved-hand.png`: Transparent single first-person right hand in a muted teal
  laboratory glove and cream/purple lab-coat cuff, holding an amber reagent
  bottle; wrist lower right, bottle upper left; crisp navy outlines, discrete
  pixel shading; whole isolated sprite, no background, labels or extra objects.
- `flask.png`: Transparent isolated front-view Erlenmeyer flask with teal liquid;
  narrow neck, broad conical body, flat base, navy pixel outlines, cream glass
  highlights, slate-blue glass and square bubbles; no stand, hands, scenery,
  labels or external shadow.

Art is decorative. Reaction outcomes are calculated by the existing engine;
the drawn liquid and room diagrams do not represent quantitative measurements.
