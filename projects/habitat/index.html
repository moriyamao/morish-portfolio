A FEW SIMPLE MOVES

Make yourself at home.

  1. Draw your shell. Choose Room and drag a rectangle, or Wall and drag a straight line. Walls join and split at intersections.
  2. Let the light in. Choose Door or Window, then click a wall with room for the opening. Doors and windows cut through the 3D wall.
  3. Make it yours. Choose furniture and click the plan. Drag to move; selected furniture has resize and rotate handles. Use Details for exact dimensions and finishes.
  4. Take a look around. Drag the 3D view to orbit; scroll or pinch to zoom. Click an object or wall to inspect it.

V select · R room · W wall · D door · N window
Arrow keys move selection 0.1 m (Shift: 0.5 m). Delete removes. Esc cancels. Ctrl/⌘ Z undoes; Shift Z redoes.

Pan the plan with Space + drag or a middle-button drag. Pinch or scroll to zoom. Use the Pan tool on touch devices. Autosave uses this browser. Download JSON for a portable backup.
